FOR THE MOMENTS IN BETWEEN

Open When… 🤍

Five little letters for five different moments. You do not have to read them all at once, pretty girl. Open the one you need.

01Open when you’re missing meA little reminder that the distance is not the whole story.＋

Hey, my girl. Missing me already? I knew I was unforgettable, but please, at least pretend to behave. 😌

I wish I could just appear beside you right now, steal a hug, annoy you for a little while and act like I did nothing. I know a message is not the same as having me there, and I will not pretend it is. But I hope this makes the distance feel a little less loud.

Think about our calls, the random things we talk about, the laughter, and all those little moments that are ours. We have already made memories worth keeping, and there are so many more waiting for us. This is just a distance to get through, not a reason to forget what we have.

So take a breath, pretty girl. I'm missing you too. Save some hugs for me, because when I finally get to see you, I am collecting every single one. And yes, you are allowed to miss me a ridiculous amount. I probably deserve it. 🤍

Yours, your favourite disturbance.

02Open when you’re sadYou do not have to pretend you’re okay here.＋

Hi, love. First, please don't feel like you have to force a smile just because someone expects you to be okay. If today is one of those days where everything feels like too much, you are allowed to admit that.

You don't have to explain every feeling perfectly, and you don't have to find the right words immediately. You can be quiet. You can cry. You can take your time. I would rather understand what you are feeling than have you pretend nothing is wrong just to make things easier for everyone else.

Please remember to be gentle with yourself, too. You spend so much time caring about other people; you deserve some of that care. Eat something if you haven't, drink some water, rest a little, and don't be hard on yourself for having a difficult day.

This moment is not your whole life, and you do not have to solve everything tonight. One small step at a time, okay? I'm in your corner, cheering for you, and I hope you never feel that you have to carry every heavy thing alone.

A very big, very patient hug from me. 🤍

03Open when you need a laughEmergency treatment for a serious case of missing my nonsense.＋

Breaking news: the most beautiful girl has been reported to be too serious. Authorities (me) have arrived to investigate. Our findings suggest that you need to smile immediately. This is not a request. 😭

I'm sure you're doing that thing where you act like you are not smiling while secretly smiling. Don't deny it. I know my audience.

Also, I would like to remind you that you willingly chose to keep someone like me in your life. Someone who will disturb you, make unnecessary jokes, ask for attention and still somehow expect an award for being cute. Your judgement is questionable, but I respect it.

In all seriousness, I hope something makes you laugh today. Even if it is this very unserious letter. You deserve light moments, silly conversations and happiness that doesn't need a reason.

Now smile for me. Yes, properly. I can't see you, but I am choosing to believe you did. Case closed. 😌

Signed, the cause of some of your headaches and hopefully many of your smiles.

04Open when you doubt yourselfFor the days you forget how much you bring to this world.＋

My Olufemi, I wish you could see yourself a little more kindly on the days when your mind starts telling you that you are not enough.

You do not have to be perfect to be worthy of good things. You do not have to have everything figured out, make everybody happy, or get everything right the first time. You are allowed to learn. You are allowed to make mistakes. You are allowed to grow at your own pace.

I see someone with a caring heart, someone who keeps trying even when things are not easy, someone with her own little ways of making a difference. Please don't reduce yourself to one bad day, one mistake, or one thing you wish you had done differently.

And when you compare yourself to other people, remember that you are not behind in your own story. You have your own timing, your own gifts and your own path. I hope you keep giving yourself the same patience you would give someone you love.

So chin up, pretty girl. Not because you must always be strong, but because you deserve to remember that you are capable, valuable and already enough. Take your time. I'm rooting for you.

Always in your corner. 🤍

05Open before you sleepOne last little note before you call it a day.＋

Hey, sleepyhead. Before you close those pretty eyes, I just want you to put today down for a moment.

Whatever went well, I'm glad you got to experience it. Whatever didn't, you can try again another day. You don't need to replay every conversation or worry about every little thing tonight. Let your mind rest. Tomorrow can wait until tomorrow.

I hope you know that you deserve peaceful nights, soft mornings and days that give you plenty of reasons to smile. I hope you remember to take care of yourself, even when you are busy taking care of everyone else.

If I were there, I'd probably bother you for a few minutes, tell you to stop scrolling and then insist that I wasn't the one keeping you awake. But since I'm not there, this letter will have to do. Imagine a forehead kiss and the warmest hug. No arguments. 😌

Sleep well, Iyawo Mi. Thank you for being part of my days. I hope you wake up feeling a little lighter and very loved.

Goodnight, my favourite girl. Sweet dreams. 🤍
